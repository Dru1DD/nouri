Nouri is a simple tracker for water, calories and your medication reminders on iPhone and Apple Watch. Drink a glass of water, raise your wrist, tap +250 — done.

  • 💊 Reminders with Taken, Snooze and Skip right in the notification
  • ⌚ Apple Watch app, complications and widgets
  • 🔒 No account, no ads, no tracking — your data stays on your devices
